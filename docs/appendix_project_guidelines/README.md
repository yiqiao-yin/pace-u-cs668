# Project Guidelines

Guidelines for completing your capstone project.

## Steps

1. **Identify a question of interest:** Choose something that interests you from industry, government, or academia. This should be a real-world problem that you will address in a comprehensive study.

2. **Design your study:** Review existing research, and figure out what data you need and how you will get it.

3. **Data collection:** You may use published data, or you may collect the data yourself. Do not download and copy an existing project! This is your own original work.

4. **Data analysis:** Perform the necessary analysis to address your question of interest. Include data descriptions and exploratory data analysis, the methods you used, and the findings. You are expected to analyze data. A descriptive summary is not sufficient.

5. **Poster:** Create a poster clearly reflecting your analysis and your findings. Follow the general format of an academic research paper.

6. **Final submission:** Submit your final poster on early December (Fall) / early May (Spring). A well-conceived study with an appropriate analysis and a clearly written poster will be more favorably graded than a poor scientific effort with a fancy analysis.

7. **In-Class Presentation:** Each student will present their findings in class in a brief presentation on the last day of classes in mid-December (Fall) / mid-May (Spring). Make sure you follow the guidelines in the syllabus for making a clear organized presentation.

## Timeline

Describe in detail the analytical methods you will use. This is not a bulleted list of the steps you'll take, but a description of the methods, showing that you understand them. |

Your graphs and tables should look professional. |

| mid-December (Fall) / mid-May (Spring) | **In-class Presentations** |

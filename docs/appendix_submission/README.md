# Appendix - Final Submission

## Project Requirement

Students must build at least one project for the semester and commit their code to a GitHub repository. This is a mandatory requirement for course completion.

## Grading and Earnings

Students must submit their GitHub repository through [wyn360search.com](https://wyn360search.com). Your grade will be determined by your earnings on the platform.

### How Earnings Work

Share your best GitHub repositories on WYN360 and get paid for your contributions! Our earnings system rewards you based on the quality and impact of your shared repos.

**To maximize your earnings potential, focus on:**

- **Community Engagement** - Stars, forks, and watchers all matter
- **Comprehensive Documentation** - Well-documented repos tend to perform better
- **Active Maintenance** - Keep your repositories updated and maintained
- **Clear Descriptions** - Help others understand what your project does

Once your repository qualifies and accumulates earnings, simply connect your Stripe account to withdraw your funds directly to your bank. The more value you bring to the WYN360 community through high-quality, well-maintained repositories, the more you can earn.

Start sharing today and turn your open-source contributions into real rewards.

## Submission Instructions

### Step 1: Add Your Repository

1. Navigate to the **Repo** page on [wyn360search.com](https://wyn360search.com)
2. Paste your GitHub repository URL in the input field
3. Click **"Add Repository"**
4. Once added, your repo will appear in your collection where you can manage and track its performance

### Step 2: Engage with the Community

- Browse other users' repositories
- Leave thoughtful comments
- Rate repos with star ratings
- Discover interesting projects

The more you participate, the more visibility your own repos can gain.

### Step 3: Maintain Quality

Keep your repositories active and well-documented on GitHub. Our system periodically checks for quality signals including:

- Repository activity
- Documentation quality
- Community engagement metrics
- Code organization

### Step 4: Withdraw Earnings

1. Click **"Connect Stripe to Withdraw"** to set up your payout account
2. Complete Stripe's secure onboarding process
3. Once connected, request withdrawals when your balance reaches the minimum threshold
